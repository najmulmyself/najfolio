Shipping is more than running `flutter build`. Here's the checklist I follow for every release.

1. **Internal QA** with Firebase App Distribution
2. **Feature flags** with Remote Config for controlled rollouts
3. **Store submission** for the Play Store and App Store
4. **Monitoring** with Crashlytics after release

```bash
flutter build appbundle --release
```

Each step catches a different class of problem before your users do.
